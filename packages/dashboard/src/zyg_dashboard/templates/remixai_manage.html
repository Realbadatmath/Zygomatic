Remix Ai [ operator console ]

Sweep for popular AI-generated video → study its format → build an original in that format. Board: /workflows → Remix Ai. Output: inspection → Videos → remix ai.

What is copied and what is not. The aesthetic and the structure are studied and reused — a look and a beat-shape are not ownable. The pictures, the sentences, the title and the music are not: images are generated, the script is written from an abstract brief (never from the source's wording), the title is written to the source's format rather than its words, and the soundtrack is replaced with a different track of the same genre from our own library. The source video is deleted as soon as analysis finishes. Nothing delivered here contains a frame, a waveform or a sentence of it. Nothing auto-publishes — each job leaves an upload sheet for you to read.
