Mesh

Mesh skeletons

Two immutable ORIGINALS (male / female) are the templates for every character body. Deriving a character copies the template through its parameter recipe — phenotype axes, 256 per-part morphs, per-bone scaling — and stores the result as that character's base. Originals are hash-verified on every load. Click a card to open it · ← → next / previous skeleton · ↑ ↓ view angle · 3D orbit = drag to rotate the real mesh with its quad grid · Esc closes.
The coloured chip is the anthropometric gate verdict (geom/anthro): how this body sits against real survey distributions, on a stated measurement convention, against a stated population. It is a measurement result, not a quality score — a FAIL means "outside the stated band by a stated amount", never "bad body", and the deliberate part of any departure belongs in the character's stylization record. Open a card and press anthro for the tier-by-tier report and the percentile strip.
