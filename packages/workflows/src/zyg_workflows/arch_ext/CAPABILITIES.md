## analyse_geometry — `arch_assistant_ext/analyse_geometry.py`

Analyse (these four only read — they never change the drawing, and each has a dock icon of the same name): clash_check(which) reports every pair of solids that occupy the same space, with the shared volume — corner joints between walls, openings in their host wall and things bearing on a slab are excused. headroom_check(min) is the clear height under every slab, flat roof and beam and over every stair tread, against 2.3 m (the Dutch new-build value; 2.0 m is the absolute Bouwbesluit floor). guarding_check(min_h, max_gap) finds every deck edge with more than a metre of drop that has no wall, railing, balustrade or parapet along it, and any railing whose members are further apart than the 100 mm sphere rule. stair_check() reports every flight against Blondel (2 x rise + run between 0.60 and 0.65 m), rise <= 0.185 m, run >= 0.22 m, width >= 0.80 m, and whether it actually lands on the next storey. press_button("a_clash" / "a_headroom" / "a_guard" / "a_stair") shows the same findings to the person as a sheet with a select button per finding.

_4 tools · 0 kinds · 4 worked examples_

### Tools (4)

- **`clash_check`** — Every pair of solids that occupy the same space, with the shared volume and how far they run into each other.
  - args: `[which]`
- **`guarding_check`** — Every slab, roof or balcony edge more than a metre above what is under it that has no wall, railing, balustrade or parapet along it, plus any railing whose members are further apart than the 100 mm sphere rule.
  - args: `[min_h, max_gap]`
- **`headroom_check`** — The clear height under every slab, flat roof and beam and over every stair tread.
  - args: `[min]`
- **`stair_check`** — Every flight against Blondel (2 x rise + run between 0.60 and 0.65 m), rise <= 0.185 m, run >= 0.22 m, width >= 0.80 m, and whether steps x rise actually lands on the next storey.
  - args: `[storey_h]`

## blender_power — `arch_assistant_ext/blender_power.py`

present() is the hand-over: ONE build, five renders — approach (28 mm on the entrance axis 30 m out), section (cut through the door axis, where a stair through slabs or a shaft without a floor opening shows), elevation, aerial and lobby (4 m inside the door looking out, stepped back if a wall is in its face) — and it answers in numbers only: records VISIBLE (by ray cast) of the records in frustum, the tallest visible one, door in frame yes/no, slabs crossed without an opening, plus every design-check finding. done requires present() after the LAST drawing change; do not judge your own picture in adjectives. blender_render(view="section", axis="x"|"z", at=cell|metres) cuts the building open on one plane and drops everything behind it; the four elevations are front/back/right/left; an INTERIOR shot is from_cell/to_cell with eye_height. Blender: blender_render(view, engine) gives you a PICTURE of the drawing rebuilt in Blender — use it to check a result you cannot judge from the parametric numbers (engine: workbench or eevee). Views: iso/top/front/back/right/left are orthographic drawings; "eye" stands on the ground at the south edge at 1.7 m and looks north (the walk the operator takes — use it to judge a landscape, a street or a facade the way a person sees it); "aerial" looks down from 45 degrees in the south-west and frames the whole lot. from_cell/to_cell stand the camera on a cell and look at another — the way to check an entrance from its approach or a roof from its deck (eye_height above the surface under from_cell, focal_mm 14..135, look_height above the surface under to_cell; verticals stay plumb). The picture has a real sky and a 35 degree south-west sun, so slopes shade and a v3 ground shows its grass/rock/sand/snow vertex colours. blender_measure() returns a table of per-object bounding boxes, mesh volumes and face counts — use it to verify sizes and to spot objects that did not build. blender_export(format) writes obj/fbx/stl/glb/usd/ply to disk and returns the path, for the user to take away. blender_run(script, render, view) runs YOUR OWN bpy Python inside that Blender: use it only for things the studio kinds cannot express — booleans, bevels, arrays, subdivision, custom meshes, material tweaks — and set render=true to get a picture back of what your script did. The script runs as the operator on this machine, so keep it to the scene; edits are SAVED into the .blend and persist to the next tool call in this request, but they do NOT flow back into the parametric drawing — the studio document is unchanged, so never use blender_run to "draw" something the user wants in the studio (use the drawing tools). Every studio record becomes one Blender object named "<kind> <id>" (e.g. "wall o7") carrying the custom property obj['studio_id']. bpy cheat-sheet: import bpy, bmesh, math, mathutils ob = bpy.data.objects['wall o7'] # by name; bpy.data.objects.keys() lists them [o for o in bpy.data.objects if o.get('studio_id') == 'o7'] # by studio id ob.location = (x, y, z) # blender frame: x = studio x, y = -studio z, z = studio y (up) ob.rotation_euler = (0, 0, math.radians(30)); ob.scale = (1, 1, 2) m = ob.modifiers.new('bev', 'BEVEL'); m.width = 0.05; m.segments = 3 m = ob.modifiers.new('arr', 'ARRAY'); m.count = 6; m.relative_offset_displace = (1.2, 0, 0) m = ob.modifiers.new('sub', 'SUBSURF'); m.levels = 2 m = ob.modifiers.new('bool', 'BOOLEAN'); m.operation = 'DIFFERENCE'; m.object = cutter bpy.ops.object.select_all(action='DESELECT'); ob.select_set(True) bpy.context.view_layer.objects.active = ob; bpy.ops.object.modifier_apply(modifier='bev') bpy.ops.mesh.primitive_cube_add(size=2, location=(0, 0, 1)) # new object = context.active_object bm = bmesh.new(); bm.from_mesh(ob.data) # bmesh: bm.verts/.edges/.faces, bm.calc_volume() bmesh.ops.inset_region(bm, faces=bm.faces[:], thickness=0.1) bm.to_mesh(ob.data); bm.free(); ob.data.update() bpy.ops.object.mode_set(mode='EDIT') # only with an active object; 'OBJECT' to come back mat = bpy.data.materials.get('mat_glass') or bpy.data.materials.new('mat_glass') ob.data.materials.clear(); ob.data.materials.append(mat) mat.use_nodes = True; mat.node_tree.nodes['Principled BSDF'].inputs['Base Color'].default_value = (1,0,0,1) bpy.data.objects.remove(ob, do_unlink=True) print(...) — everything you print comes back to you as the tool result.

## circulation_rules — `arch_assistant_ext/circulation_rules.py`

CIRCULATION: every floor with a slab must be reached by a stair, and by a lift from 3 storeys up (a shaft has a door on every served storey); the roof deck counts as a floor when anything but roof plant stands on it.
