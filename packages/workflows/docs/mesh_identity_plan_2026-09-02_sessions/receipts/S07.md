## 2. MediaPipe-478 → GNM correspondence — `geom/head_fit.py corr`

Cached at `data/assets/ict_facekit/mp468_to_ibug68.json`. Also cached:
`canonical_face_model.obj` (fetched from MediaPipe, Apache-2.0) and
`mediapipe_facemesh_sets.json` (the FACEMESH index sets; the installed mediapipe 0.10.35
ships only the tasks API with no `mediapipe.python.solutions`, so the sets were taken from
the upstream Apache-2.0 source and the provenance says so).

**Method — derived, not recalled.** Both ICT (`idx_to_landmark_verts`) and GNM
(`landmark_points`) ship 68 landmarks in the iBUG ordering, and all three meshes were
*measured* to share the X-right / Y-up / Z-forward layout (`_frame_check` raises rather
than fitting a mirrored face). Eight anchors are found as extrema inside the relevant
FACEMESH set (nose tip, chin, four eye corners, two mouth corners) → Umeyama similarity →
greedy unique nearest neighbour for each iBUG landmark **inside its own FACEMESH set**
(jaw→FACE_OVAL, brows→eyebrow sets, nose→NOSE, eyes→eye sets, mouth→LIPS) → similarity
refit on all 68. The eight anchors landed on MP indices 4, 152, 33, 133, 362, 263, 61, 291
— the published MediaPipe semantics for exactly those points, arrived at geometrically.

**Correspondence table: 68 entries, injective. Residual 5.17 mm rms**, by group:

| group | rms mm |
|---|---|
| FACE_OVAL (jaw 0–16) | **7.50** |
| eyebrows | 5.39 |
| nose | 4.13 |
| eyes | **3.04** |
| lips | 3.93 |

**ICT-68 vs GNM-68 ordering check, with its null.** On landmarks 17–67: Procrustes
**3.68 mm** against a null of 20 shuffled orderings at **44.42 mm** mean / 43.73 best.
Same ordering, unambiguously.

**JAWLINE FINDING (this is why `JAW_WEIGHT = 0.0`).** On the full 68 the two models sit
18.43 mm apart, and that number is made almost entirely of iBUG 0–16: **the jaw alone is
35.5 mm rms, 84.7 mm max**. GNM and ICT do not anchor "jawline" to the same anatomy.
A MediaPipe FACE_OVAL point steered at a GNM jaw anchor therefore carries a systematic
error of that size, so the jawline is excluded from the objective. This was measured and
then ablated (§3), not argued.

## 3. The fit — `geom/head_fit.py run`

Fitted per character: GNM identity (253) + expression (**64 of the 383** components — the
rest move no landmark by more than 0.2 mm and are unidentifiable from 68 points, so they
are not free parameters) + global rotation + pinhole camera `[tz, cx, cy]` with
f = 1.35·max(W,H), the anny_fit lens convention (`anny_fit.py:324`) adapted to GNM's
Y-up/Z-forward frame. Joint identity over a character's frontal + three-quarter cells,
then held-out evaluation.

**The null:** the mean head (identity = 0) refitted on the *same held-out cells* with its
own camera, rotation and expression — equally free in everything except identity. Gain is
null − fit and nothing else is ever called identity capacity.

### Held-out result (iBUG 17–67, the landmarks the objective actually uses)

| character | held-out cells | fit mm | NULL (mean head) mm | gain mm | gain % | cells improved | \|id\|max | sat>2.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c3345deb5bde (Abel) | 15 | **2.12** | 3.75 | **+1.63** | 43.5 | 15/15 | 3.06 | 2 |
| 0015c8a3f2e3 (Cornelis) | 12 | **2.20** | 3.78 | **+1.58** | 41.8 | 12/12 | 2.63 | 1 |

All-68 numbers, carried for completeness but **not** the headline (17 of those landmarks
were never optimised): Abel 16.60 mm fit vs 18.69 null; Cornelis 16.90 vs 17.62.

Four-state ledger: Abel 43 fitted / 5 no_face / 0 declined / 0 failed (48 cells);
Cornelis 35 / 12 / 0 / 0 (47 cells). No cell exceeded the |yaw| > 1.05 rad decline limit.

### Saturation

Abel reaches **|id|max 3.06 — past GNM's documented ±3 wall** with 2 coefficients beyond
2.5; Cornelis stays inside at 2.63 with 1. Abel touching the wall is the same tell
`gnm_fit.clip_hits` was built for and is consistent with a 12-year-old head sitting at the
edge of a space built from adult scans.

### Jaw-weight ablation (`--jaw-weight 0.25`) — a bigger number that means less

| character | jawW | fit mm | null mm | gain mm | \|id\|max | sat>2.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abel | 0.00 | 2.12 | 3.75 | +1.63 | 3.06 | 2 |
| Abel | 0.25 | 2.10 | 4.44 | +2.34 | **5.63** | **25** |
| Cornelis | 0.00 | 2.20 | 3.78 | +1.58 | 2.63 | 1 |
| Cornelis | 0.25 | 2.27 | 4.10 | +1.83 | **5.19** | **16** |

Reading it honestly: letting the jaw in barely changes the **fit** (2.10 vs 2.12) — it
degrades the **null** (4.44 vs 3.75), because the null is dragged by a correspondence that
is systematically wrong. The larger "gain" is a handicapped control, bought by ramming
identity coefficients to 5.6 — nearly twice the documented range, with 25 coefficients past
2.5. `JAW_WEIGHT = 0.0` stands on these numbers.

### Two solver bugs found and fixed by measurement, both recorded in the module

1. **Camera scales.** cx/cy (hundreds of px) and tz (~1 m) in one Adam group → 127 px rms.
   Everything is now normalised (tz = tz0·exp(log_tz), c = c0 + dc·f, c0 centroid-matched),
   so one learning rate is correct for every parameter.
2. **A silently disabled expression channel.** With the residual divided by f and
   hand-picked prior weights, the expression prior outweighed the data term ~150× and
   expression came back at L2 0.001 on *every* cell. Replaced with an explicit MAP
   objective: `Σ w·‖proj−target‖²/(2σ²) + ½‖id‖² + ½‖ex‖²`, where the unit-normal priors
   are GNM's own documented coefficient statistics (nothing tuned) and **σ is the measured
   5.17 mm correspondence residual converted to px through each cell's own depth**.

Also vectorised: the per-cell Python loop cost 54 s for one cell (≈75 min for the three
solves a character needs). The batched projection returns bit-identical numbers (7.548 px,
expression L2 1.924 on cell_00, both versions) and a character now takes ~140 s.

Outputs: `output/head_fit/<char>/fit.json`, `fit_jaw0.25.json`, fitted GLBs,
`output/head_fit/summary.json`.

## 4. Expression plates — `geom/head_fit.py plates`

`output/head_fit/plates/A_control/` — 70 PNGs + `plates.json` + `driver.json`.
Driver: `output/multichar_seated/abel_pose_probe/A_control.mp4`, stride 2, 40 frames.

ICT (not GNM) drives these, exactly for the reason the brief gives: GNM's 383 expression
components are unnamed per-region PCA, ICT's 53 carry the ARKit names MediaPipe already
emits, so **no expression fit happens at all** — the blendshape score *is* the weight and
only the 6-dof rigid pose is solved. MediaPipe runs in the server venv as a subprocess
(it is deliberately not on 3.12); everything after is numpy.

Face region = ICT's own `idx_to_fitting_verts` (7801 verts, z −1.8…13.1 cm), which
excludes the back of the skull (z to −9.2) so the occiput cannot enter the depth ramp.
Conventions per `geom/conditioning.py:25-27, :132`: depth near = white normalised over the
face only; normal (n·0.5+0.5)·255 camera-space, background (128,128,255).

**Measured:** 35 plated / 5 no_face / 0 declined / 0 failed. Landmark reprojection
**5.02 px rms, 2.05 px excluding the jaw**. Interior holes **0.68% mean, 0.90% max**;
bbox fill 0.64 (a face is not a rectangle — that one is not a defect and is reported
separately so it cannot be mistaken for one); 16.4 samples per covered pixel.

Eyeballed on the contact sheet: depth reads as a solid face with the nose nearest;
normals are correct camera-space (lavender background, blue toward viewer, red/pink on
the side-facing cheek); eyelids and lips track the driver.

**Rasteriser defect found and fixed by looking.** The first version drew a fixed 4 samples
per triangle and the plates came back with **12.6% salt-and-pepper holes**, concentrated
exactly where ICT's topology is coarse (scalp, cheek, neck) while lips and eyes were
oversampled. Sampling is now proportional to *projected* area, so coverage is uniform in
image space: holes 12.6% → **0.68%**, coverage +23%. The hole metric was also wrong — it
counted the whole bounding box, conflating face shape with rasteriser failure; it now
reports `bbox_fill` and `interior_holes` (a 5×5 morphological close) separately.

Caveat for S01/S08: this driver's face is only ~97 px wide, so the blendshape signal is
weak and frame-to-frame expression change is subtle. The plate geometry is sound; a
closer-framed driver would exercise the expression channel harder.

## 5. The bridge decision — REFUSED, with numbers

`output/head_fit/bridge/bridge_decision.json`. Built as a **delta on anny vertices**, never
a mesh swap, so topology and the registry cage-hash contract survive either answer
(`topology_preserved: true`, 13718 verts unchanged, on all four bodies). The registry was
not touched — S04 owns it.

Rule: BUILD only where `identity_gain_mm > seam_error_rms_mm`, per body.
Seam error := `(1 − blend_weight)·|delta|` over the blend band — the displacement the
Laplacian falloff discards, i.e. real geometry the transfer replaces with a smooth ramp.
Crop per `gnm_fit.py:50-57`, chin cut `:184-190`; blend weight is 1 on the head core
(3180 verts), 0 off the head (9550), Laplacian-smoothed over an 882-vertex band.

| body | identity gain mm | seam error rms mm | seam max mm | core delta rms mm | ratio | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| male | +1.97 | 6.04 | 12.44 | 11.01 | 0.33 | REFUSE |
| female | +1.76 | 5.93 | 12.15 | 11.12 | 0.30 | REFUSE |
| abel | +0.94 | 3.37 | 7.84 | 5.88 | 0.28 | REFUSE |
| cornelis | +2.09 | 5.40 | 11.35 | 10.25 | 0.39 | REFUSE |

**Unanimous REFUSE. No `anatomy_layer` key was written.** The transfer costs 2.5–3.5× more
than it delivers on every registered body.

The verdict survives substituting the *other* gain measurement: this table uses the Phase-1
mesh-ICP gain over the mean head (`data/assets/gnm_head/fits/<body>/fit.json`, 2026-08-21).
Using instead the image-based held-out gain measured here (Abel +1.63, Cornelis +1.58 mm)
changes no verdict — every gain number available is below every seam error.

**A second number that should stop this cold even if the ratio improved:**
`nn_transfer_rms_mm` is **12.2–15.6 mm**. That is the nearest-neighbour distance from anny
head vertices to the fitted GNM surface — the correspondence the transfer rides on is
itself 12–16 mm loose, so the delta would not even land on the matching anatomy. Any future
attempt needs a real dense correspondence (registration, not nearest neighbour) *before*
the ratio is worth re-measuring.

- **No `anatomy_layer` key, no registry write.** The measurement said REFUSE; the brief
  says write the reason and stop. S04 owns the registry regardless.
- **Only 2 characters fitted** (the only two with `output/char_mesh/` cells). `male` and
  `female` have no contact sheet, so the image-based fit has nothing to fit them from;
  they appear in the bridge table via their Phase-1 mesh fits only.
- **Plates on one driver clip.** `A_control.mp4` was the only candidate with a face on
  most frames (7/9 sampled vs 2/9 and 3/9 for the two `scene20_cut4` shots).
- **No Blender turntables of the fitted heads.** The GLBs are written; rendering them adds
  nothing the held-out numbers do not already say, and the GPU belongs to S01/S06/S08.
- **No tensorflow, no `mesh_skeletons.py` edit, no FLAME/MICA/EMOCA/SMIRK/BFM fetch** —
  the last is registered as an explicit DENY instead.
- **The `_neutral` MediaPipe channel is dropped**, not mapped: it is a confidence, not a
  shape.
