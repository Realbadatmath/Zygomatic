## What existed before

spread alarm calibrated on a **single render** (`identity_bank.py:611-614` as it was), and
had **zero callers** — `grep check_clip(` found only its own `__main__`.

**The impostor IS separated, so the metric is usable.** Worst case is Cornelis' bank on
Abel's clip: arm mean 0.3404 against a worst genuine arm mean of 0.5240 — a gap of
**0.184** at the arm level and 0.185 at p10. The two *same-clip* impostor arms (the other
character's column of the very same two-hander: identical style, grade, framing, render,
LoRA pair) score 0.146 and 0.175 — the hardest impostor available is **not** the closest

Three things the bench says that a single-render calibration could not:

* **The per-frame gap is razor thin.** Worst genuine frame 0.3943, best impostor frame
  0.3763 — an overlap-free window only **0.018** wide. No single frame may decide a clip.
  This is why the verdict is a *fraction of frames*, not `all(frames)`.
* **Shuffle lands on top of true**: 0.7656 vs 0.7666 (Abel), 0.5319 vs 0.5240 (Cornelis).
  Expected for an order-blind metric, and it is the proof that the summary statistics carry
  **no temporal information**. `spread` is therefore dispersion, never continuity.
* **The two characters are not equally safe.** Abel sits at 0.73-0.82, Cornelis at
  0.50-0.57. Both pass; Cornelis has ~0.08 of headroom and Abel ~0.31. Whatever trips first
  will be Cornelis, and that may mean his 3-image bank is a worse centroid rather than that
  his identity moved. Enrol more before tightening.
* **Ceiling is IN-BANK** (the canon portrait is one of the three enrolled images), so
  0.93-0.95 is the top of the achievable scale plus an h264 round trip, not an independent
  genuine sample. Stated so nobody reads it as a genuine-arm number.

### Chosen floors and their derivation

Written into `identity_bank.CLIP_FLOORS` / `CLIP_PASS_FRAC` / `CLIP_MIN_FOUND` /
`CLIP_SPREAD_ALARM`, each with the derivation in a comment beside it.

| knob | value | derivation |
|---|---|---|
| per-frame clip floor, facenet | **0.42** | Genuine arms hold pass_frac >= 0.98 anywhere in [0.40, 0.45]; every impostor arm is at 0.000 from 0.38 up. 0.42 is the middle of that plateau: +0.044 over the highest impostor frame (0.3763), and 0.078 of headroom under Cornelis' worst genuine frame (0.498). 0.45 would buy +0.074 impostor margin but cut Cornelis to 0.048. |
| clip pass_frac floor | **0.70** | Genuine arms measured 0.980-1.000, impostor arms 0.000-0.000 at floor 0.42. 0.70 sits 0.28 under the worst genuine and 0.70 over the best impostor. The aggregate is what absorbs the 0.018-wide per-frame gap. |
| min found frames for a verdict | **8** | Measured: at stride 16 a 3.7 s clip gives 6 found frames, so pass_frac moves in steps of 0.167 and one frame crosses the 0.70 line. Under 8 found frames the numbers are reported and `ok` is `None`. Every bench arm had >= 15 found, so this does not touch the calibration. |
| spread alarm | **0.35**, informational | Spread **separates nothing**: genuine 0.003-0.299, impostor 0.085-0.350 — the ranges overlap. Explicitly NOT part of `ok`. 0.35 sits just above the largest genuine spread as a "go and look" flag. |
| per-frame clip floor, dinov2 | 0.50, **UNBENCHED** | Both benched characters have facenet banks, so no arm ran on dinov2. The still-image floors (`_FLOORS`, facenet 0.35) are **untouched** — other callers of
`check()` depend on them and a per-frame clip floor is a different measurement.

### Legacy vs per-frame

Legacy on the same Abel clip: mean 0.7736 over 5 windows, verdict "ok". Not wrong —
**blind**. It sampled 5 moments of a 1411-frame clip and all 5 landed inside a single shot.
The per-frame instrument found 34 measurable frames and, over the same clip, 20 `not_found`
and 5 `not_detectable` — the old instrument had no state for either. Kept behind
`mode='legacy'`, verified still working (`measured=5/5, cos=0.7612`).

## 2. The instrument (`identity_bank.py`)

* `track_face_box(frame_path, roi=None, margin=0.45)` — per-frame face box from the
  MediaPipe cascade (`geom/keypoints.detect`), returned in original-frame fractions and
  clamped to the ROI. The ROI is what says *which* character to score in a two-hander (the
  only job the old static box actually did); inside it the box is re-derived every frame,
  so the character may walk, lean and turn.
* **Four-state ledger, one state per sampled frame**, never averaged together:
  `found` (scored) / `not_found` (no subject in frame at all — a cutaway) /
  `not_detectable` (a body is there but no face resolves: rear view, extreme pose, or the
  embedder rejects a face the tracker saw — **not** an identity failure) /
  `unmeasurable` (our machinery failed; never reads as a pass).
* `check_clip(..., mode='perframe'|'legacy', stride=4, roi=...)` — one ffmpeg pass for all
  sampled frames (a per-frame seek costs ~0.4 s each), returns the per-frame `series` plus
  the ledger and the aggregates. `ok` is `None` whenever nothing was found or too few
  frames were found. Records **one** row per clip, not one per frame — 350 per-frame rows
  would turn a within-clip wobble into a cross-episode "ratchet" that never happened.
* `enabled()` — `ZYG_IDENTITY_CLIP`, default on, advisory everywhere.

## 3. Where it is wired (all advisory; nothing fails a pipeline)

* **`pipeline_debug.qc_clip(..., identity_chars=, identity_style=, identity_stride=)`** →
  result in `out['identity']` and in `identity_clip.json` next to the clip.
  It is deliberately **kept out of `out['checks']`**: that list is the run's pass/fail
  ledger, and a metric benched for the first time today has not earned a vote in it.
* **`arcane_multichar.py`** (`_multichar_clip` QC block) passes the cast with **full-height
  column ROIs** — not the existing motion regions, which start at y=0.30 and would hand the
  face tracker a chin — and logs one `[multichar][identity]` line per character with the
  full ledger.
* **`identity_bank.score_run(run_dir)`** — the plain function S05's Movement Studio calls on
  a finished job. Discovers the cast from the run's own json when not told, gives each
  character an equal-column ROI in order (the same convention `arcane_multichar` uses),
  writes `identity_clip.json`, returns a summary. `bp_movement.py` was **not** touched.
* CLI: `python identity_bank.py check-clip <id> --path <clip> [--mode legacy] [--stride N]`
  and `python identity_bank.py score-run --path <run_dir>`.

**Verified on the real path**, not just imported:
`qc_clip` on `output/multichar_seated/FINAL_seated_alive_1664x960.mp4` with both characters
and column ROIs → Cornelis cos 0.5666 / p10 0.5044 / pass_frac 1.0 → pass; Abel cos 0.4188 /
p10 0.3529 / pass_frac 0.667 → below_pass_frac. Sidecar written; `checks` list untouched
(10 entries, none of them identity).

### A defect this found in its own zero-config path
`score_run` on `output/multichar_seated` discovered **one** character from the run json, so
no ROI was applied, the whole-frame detector locked onto whichever face it preferred, and
one frame of five scored **-0.047** — Abel's face against Cornelis' bank. A discovered cast
of one is a *guess* that the clip is a solo shot. `score_run` now reports `chars_source` and
attaches an explicit `caution` when a single character was auto-discovered, and logs it.
Callers that know their cast (arcane_multichar does) pass `chars=` and are unaffected.

## 4. The restores score themselves

* **`wan_face_restore.score_identity_delta(clip_in, clip_out, char_ids, rois=)`** — per-frame
  identity before and after. `restore_faces` now encodes to `<out>.stage.mp4`, scores it,
  writes `<out_path>.identity.json`, and only then `os.replace`s into place — so a lost-
  identity restore can never land on the caller's clip. With
  `ZYG_FACE_RESTORE_REQUIRE_GAIN=1` it deletes the staged file and returns `None`
  (fail-safe: the caller keeps its input). An **unmeasurable** delta (`ok is None`) never
  blocks.
* **`wan_prop_restore.score_prop_delta(...)`** — same shape for props: DINOv2 CLS of the prop
  box against the prop reference, before and after, sidecar `<out_path>.identity.json`, and
  `ZYG_PROP_RESTORE_REQUIRE_GAIN=1` to refuse a loss. Same staging discipline.

**Measured delta on real clips** (`FINAL_seated_vace_dualLoRA.mp4` -> `FINAL_seated_alive_1664x960.mp4`,
stride 12, both characters, column ROIs):

measurement of a face-restore operation.

`creature_identity.clip_similarity(clip, anchor, band=, stride=, roi=)` — DINOv2 CLS

Complete separation: worst genuine frame 0.6669 against best impostor frame 0.1420 — a gap

**Honest limit, written into the docstring:** there is no creature *detector*, so every
extractable frame is `found` and `not_found`/`not_detectable` are 0 by construction. A frame
the creature has walked out of scores low and is indistinguishable from a frame with the
wrong creature in it. Until a creature detector exists this answers "does this frame look
like the anchor", not "is the right creature present". The same limit applies to
`score_prop_delta` (fixed box, no prop detector) and is stated there too.

## Not done, and why

* **No VACE face-restore or prop-restore job was actually run.** That is a heavy GPU render
  (25 steps, 89 frames at 1664x960) and this task is queued as light; other sessions may be
  on the GPU. The delta function, the sidecar, the staging and the refusal branch are all
  exercised on real clips and real values, but no end-to-end `restore_faces()` call
  produced a before/after pair. **Next person: run one under the flock lock and confirm the
  sidecar lands and the refusal fires.**
* **No dinov2 clip arm.** Both benched characters have facenet banks, so the dinov2 clip
  floor (0.50) is inherited, not measured, and is flagged as such in code and in every
  result (`floor_benched`). A dinov2 clip verdict must not be read as an identity verdict.
* **The bench cast is two characters.** Every impostor number comes from Abel-vs-Cornelis.
  A wider cast would tell us whether 0.42 generalises; two characters cannot.
* **`spread` gates nothing** and I did not try to make it. It does not separate and the
  shuffle arm explains why. Left as an informational flag rather than deleted, because the
  dispersion number is still worth eyeballing.
* **Nothing was promoted into the pass/fail ledger.** Advisory only, per the brief.
