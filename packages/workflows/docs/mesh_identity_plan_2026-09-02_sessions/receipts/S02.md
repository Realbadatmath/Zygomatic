## 1. The split-brain is closed: fits and plates now build from the registry

**What was wrong.** `anny_fit`, `anny_animate` and `body_grid` all built their body from
`phenotype.calibrate()` — three numbers solving height for a declared age. A registered
body is four things (phenotype, `local_changes`, `bone_scales` including the recorded
phalanx correction, and `anatomy_layer`), and three of the four never reached a fit.

**What was built.** `geom/phenotype.py` gained the registry-body resolver: `resolve_body_name`,
`build_registry_cage`, `registry_body`, `body_model`, `bone_scale_matrix`, `forward_kwargs`,
`legacy_spec`, `spec_for`, `describe`, `ANATOMY_LAYER_NOTE`. `build_registry_cage` follows the
`Subject.from_registry` sequence exactly (`_build_mesh(params=)` → `_apply_anatomy`) and
**refuses** rather than warns on a hash mismatch.

Two things had to be right for a posed forward to carry the registered body:

* **The model.** A registry body is driven through `mesh_skeletons._model()`
  (`local_changes='all'`). On the bare model `anny_fit` used, every local morph is silently
  ignored — for cornelis that is 30 morphs discarded without a word.
* **The pose matrix.** anny takes one `(1,N,4,4)` block per bone and both the pose rotation
  and the bone scale want it. They compose as `M = R @ S`. Verified by its two degenerate
  cases, which is why it is a derivation and not a choice:

```
abel     forward_kwargs vs _build_mesh: max delta 0.000e+00 m
         zero-pose vs rest:             max delta 0.000e+00 m
cornelis forward_kwargs vs _build_mesh: max delta 0.000e+00 m
         zero-pose vs rest:             max delta 0.000e+00 m
```

**Hash assertion output (run after the last edit):**

```
cage abel      rebuilt 3d5159da52ec7d43 recorded 3d5159da52ec7d43 MATCH
cage cornelis  rebuilt 9b38e7729367fccf recorded 9b38e7729367fccf MATCH
```

**CLI.** `anny_fit` and `anny_animate` both take `--body <registry name|char_id|asset>`,
`--legacy-phenotype`, and (fit) `--out-name`, (animate) `--ctrl-name`, `--pose-char`,
`--fit-name`. **The default changed**: a `char_id` that resolves to a registered body now
fits with that body, and says so on stdout. `body_grid._project` no longer re-derives a
body at all — it reads the one the fit recorded and refuses if that body's cage no longer
rebuilds to the hash the fit was made against.

**What is NOT carried.** `anatomy_layer` is a rest-space vertex layer applied after the
forward pass; its relief component lives in the REST bone frame and would be applied in the
wrong frame once posed. It is verified on the rest cage (it is inside the hash) and is not
re-applied to posed frames. `geom.phenotype.ANATOMY_LAYER_NOTE` states this, every fit
report carries `body.anatomy_layer_applied: false`, and the size of the omission is
measured rather than asserted: **abel max vertex displacement 0.68 mm over 2548/13718
verts; cornelis 21.26 mm over 150/13718 (`abs_definition`)** — both under the depth-render
pixel at the scales these plates are drawn at.

## 2. Re-fit and compare — legacy versus the registered body

Every pre-existing `anny_fit.json` was copied to `anny_fit_legacy.json` first (**87 files**,
nothing replaced silently). Both characters were then re-fitted with `--body`.

```
abel     fitted 35/48 | PASSED GATE 35 (full n=6 med 7.1px | upper n=29 med 15.1px) | gated 0 | declined 13
cornelis fitted 37/47 | PASSED GATE 36 (full n=2 med 4.4px | upper n=34 med 7.2px)  | gated 1 | declined 10
```

The comparison below covers **only the cells whose `anny_fit.json` actually carries a
registry body**. The first pass of the comparison did not, counted the untouched cells in
both columns, and diluted every number toward "no change" — that table is wrong and is not
reported here.

### The phenotype actually used

| | legacy (on the 35/37 re-fitted cells) | registry |
|---|---|---|
| abel | **no phenotype at all** on 35/35 → the anny **default adult female** | `gender 0.0, age 0.2292, height 0.8873, muscle/weight/proportions 0.5` |
| cornelis | **no phenotype at all** on 37/37 → the anny **default adult female** | `gender 0.0, age 0.6385, height 0.4698, …` + **30 local morphs** |

Corpus-wide, before the re-fit, only **9 of 44** abel fits and **6 of 43** cornelis fits
carried any phenotype, and those nine/six carried `gender 1.0, height 0.9702` (abel) and
`gender 1.0, height 0.5606` (cornelis) — female macro, wrong height. Every one of those
nine/six sits among the drift-quarantined cells, so on the cells that could be re-fitted the
legacy body was not merely wrong, it was **unset**.

### Reprojection error (px, real detections only)

| char | mode | arm | n | median | mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| abel | full | legacy | 6 | 5.17 | 5.57 |
| abel | full | **registry** | 6 | **7.14** | 7.43 |
| abel | upper | legacy | 29 | 10.60 | 10.43 |
| abel | upper | **registry** | 29 | **15.05** | 14.37 |
| cornelis | full | legacy | 2 | 5.95 | 5.95 |
| cornelis | full | **registry** | 2 | **4.42** | 4.42 |
| cornelis | upper | legacy | 34 | 8.20 | 9.07 |
| cornelis | upper | **registry** | 34 | **7.20** | 8.32 |

Paired, same mode, both passing the gate:

* **abel: median +3.96 px, mean +3.58 px — 3 improved, 32 worsened.**
* **cornelis: median −1.16 px, mean −0.80 px — 24 improved, 12 worsened.**

No fit-mode changed and no cell flipped the gate, in either direction, for either character.

**Read this straight: the correct body made abel's fits WORSE.** Reprojection error grades a
body against 2D evidence, and the anny default adult female explains abel's drawn keypoints
better than the registered 12-year-old male does. That is not an argument for going back —
the legacy arm was fitting a body nobody declared, and a lower error obtained with the wrong
person is not a result. It is a measurement that the drawn Abel's proportions differ from
the registered Abel's, which is precisely the question section 4 was built to test.
Cornelis moves the other way, and his 30 local morphs are why (section 4, null B).

## 4. Shape from the sheet — `geom/shape_fit.py`

Free: `muscle`, `weight`, `proportions` within ±0.3 of declared, and eight `measure-*`
locals within ±0.8. **Fixed: age, height, gender.** Shape is shared across cells; camera and
global yaw are per-cell nuisance parameters re-solved alongside it (and identically for the
nulls); pose is frozen at the `anny_fit` value. A third of cells held out. Minibatch of 10
cells per step, 250 steps.

**Two evidence channels, and one of them mostly declined itself.** The person mask
(`regions.png`) is the **clothed** character, so the naked body sits inside it. On a cell
whose fit already lands on the drawing that residual is about build (abel `cell_24`: median
signed distance **−9.4 px**, 15 % of outline points outside). On an upper-mode cell it is
not — with only head and shoulders pinned, the body below projects wherever the prior left
it (`cell_00` **+337 px**, 84 % outside; `cell_08` **+360 px**, 86 % outside). Fed to one
objective the second kind dominated: the silhouette term was ~170 against a keypoint term
of ~10, and the first trial solve **diverged from 13.66 px to 75.22 px train error while
improving its own loss**. So a cell earns its silhouette term only if, at the declared body,
its outline already lies within 40 px of the drawn outline. **Qualified: abel 7 of 35,
cornelis 3 of 36.** The rest contribute keypoints only.

Cells whose `anny_fit.json` is still a pre-refit report are excluded — their poses were
solved against a different body, and some predate the pinhole camera (that is how the
exclusion was found: `IndexError` on `cam[3]` of a 3-element orthographic camera).

### Held-out results, against both nulls

| char | arm | n | median reproj px | median silhouette IoU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| abel | **fit** | 11 | **13.93** | **0.5956** |
| abel | null A — declared body, do nothing | 11 | 13.59 | 0.6115 |
| abel | null B — mean anny at declared age/height | 11 | 13.59 | 0.6115 |
| cornelis | **fit** | 12 | **6.81** | **0.5944** |
| cornelis | null A — declared body, do nothing | 12 | 7.01 | 0.6009 |
| cornelis | null B — mean anny at declared age/height | 12 | 19.18 | 0.6168 |

(train-arm, for reference: abel 11.56 px / 0.5908 over 24 cells; cornelis 5.53 px / 0.6117.)

**Abel's two nulls are one null.** The registered abel is `muscle/weight/proportions = 0.5`
with **zero** local morphs, which *is* the mean anny body at his declared age and height.
The two null rows are identical because they are the same body. The code checks this
numerically and records `gate.nulls_are_the_same_body: true` rather than letting the receipt
imply two independent tests. For cornelis they are genuinely different, and the gap is the
strongest number in this receipt: **19.18 px → 7.01 px, a 64 % reduction, from his 30
recorded local morphs alone.** The registry body is not decoration.

### GATE: not detectable, for both characters

> the sheet does NOT carry shape information beyond the declared build — the solved deltas
> do not beat doing nothing on held-out cells, so nothing here should be baked

* abel: loses on both axes (13.93 vs 13.59 px; 0.5956 vs 0.6115 IoU).
* cornelis: **wins on reprojection** (6.81 vs 7.01 and 19.18) and **loses on IoU** (0.5944 vs
  0.6009). The gate requires both. One channel out of two is not a result.

No axis hit its cap (`gate.saturated_axes` empty), though abel's came close — waist +0.771,
thigh +0.782, upper-arm +0.770 against the 0.8 bound. The solved abel is a visibly heavier,
stockier boy; **the side views were rendered and looked at** and show no swayback-style
profile deformation in either character — the failure is generalisation, not a wrecked
profile. Renders: `output/char_mesh/<cid>/shape_fit/<name>_{before,after,null_mean}.png`,
front and side side-by-side in each.

**Candidates written (deltas only, nothing baked):**

## Not done, and why

* **`anatomy_layer` is not applied to posed frames.** Rest-frame relief in a posed frame
  would be wrong. Measured above (0.68 mm / 21.26 mm max on the rest cage) and declared in
  every report; not hidden.
* **13 abel cells and 10 cornelis cells were not re-fitted** — 20 are drift-quarantined
  (source image gone), 2 declined for want of evidence (abel `cell_19`), 1 cornelis fit was
  gated. Their `anny_fit.json` is still the pre-refit report. Not deleted (they are
  evidence); indexed instead, in `fit_body_index.json`.
* **The shape solve froze pose.** A full joint re-fit of pose *and* shape was out of budget
  (one 87-cell fit pass is ~55 min of CPU). Camera and yaw were re-solved for every arm
  including the nulls, so the comparison is between bodies; a pose that had absorbed shape
  error could still flatter the null, which would make the gate conservative, not generous.
* **No registry reissue, no recipe edited, no number retyped.** Every value in the candidate
  files is written programmatically from the solve.
